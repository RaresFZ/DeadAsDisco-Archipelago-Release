# Dead as Disco Archipelago 0.5.10

Play **Dead as Disco** as an [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) multiworld randomizer, alone or with friends (and with any other
Archipelago game). Download **`DeadAsDiscoAP-0.5.10-windows.zip`** below, extract it and follow the `README.md` inside. No Python and no
Archipelago install are needed to *play*; only the person who generates the multiworld needs Archipelago.

> The "Source code" archives GitHub adds to this page are the repository, not the player package. Players want the `DeadAsDiscoAP-0.5.10-windows.zip`.

**Needs:** Windows 10/11, Dead as Disco on Steam at build **25772865** (the current one) or **25647873** (the app refuses other builds), Archipelago **0.6.8** for hosting.

## What the randomizer does

- **The skill tree is an Archipelago shop.** Buying a node costs fans as usual and sends a *check*; the node's vanilla reward is
  withheld. Your own skills arrive when the multiworld sends them and are equipped automatically.
- **Idol powers are shuffled.** Beating an Idol sends a check; its power arrives from the multiworld. A power you have not received
  cannot be equipped or used.
- **Missions are locked behind Access items.** You start with one mission of your choice (Hemlock by default); the other four open when
  their Access item arrives.
- **Everything you do can be a check**, and everything you receive can come from anyone's world. Filler, traps and DeathLink included.

## Checks (locations)

1164 candidate records exist; **1103 can be checks**, 11 are free at New Game and cannot be, and 50 song-related records are blocked.
A default seed has roughly **500 checks**; the options below scale that down or up (up to 1103 with songs).

| Category | Max | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| Story missions | 5 | Beating each Idol's mission (Hemlock, Arora, Dex, Prophet, Dahlia) |
| Star ratings | 530 | Reaching 1-5 stars on missions (you choose which thresholds) |
| Difficulty completions | 164 | Beating a mission on exact Easy / Normal / Hard / Very Hard |
| Challenges | 65 | Completing song challenges |
| Memorabilia | 148 | Collectibles; most count the moment you **pick them up** (hub or level), the rest when bought |
| Skill and power nodes | 12 | Skill-tree nodes of active skills and the Idol power awards |
| Passive upgrade nodes | 29 | The 29 passive upgrade nodes |
| Cosmetic purchases | 55 | Clothing bought in the shop (the clothing itself stays vanilla) |
| Dance purchases | 20 | Dance moves bought |
| Quests | 12 | Character item quests turned in |
| Achievements and tutorial | 27 | The game's achievements and finishing the tutorial |
| Infinite Disco songs (opt-in) | 36 | Completing each shipped song, plus their star and difficulty milestones |

Each category has an on/off option, and the big ones also have a percentage option (keeps a seeded random share), so you can build a short
or a very long seed. Checks that cannot be earned with your other options are never generated (for example, no quest check if quests are off).

## Items

57 item types:

| Items | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Idol powers | 6 | Phantom Dance, Thunderstruck, Singularity, Fever Rush, Show Stopper, Bass Invader |
| Active skills | 9 | Heavy Kick, Windmill, Drumstick Toss, Backup Sticks, Rush Down, Super Singularity, Super Bass Invader, and the free starters Double Time and Let's Dance |
| Passive upgrades | 29 | Stalwart Heart, Disco Inferno, Life Support, Perfect Pitch, Event Horizon and the rest of the tree |
| Mission Access | 5 | One per Idol mission |
| Fan Packs | 5 | Fans for the skill-tree shop: **125 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000** at the default pack size (weighted toward small and normal) |
| Traps | 2 | **Half Heart** (lose a little health, never lethal) and **Silence** (run, but no attacking, dodging or abilities for a few seconds) |
| Disco Hint | 1 | Only if you lower the Fan Pack percentage; off by default |

The three free starter skills (Double Time, Fever Rush, Let's Dance) are your start inventory. Skills, powers, upgrades and Access items are
real progression/useful items; Fan Packs are filler; traps replace part of the surplus filler and never replace progression.

## DeathLink

Optional (off by default). An incoming DeathLink drains your health in small hits and then the normal death screen appears, with the usual
Retry. Your own death sends one outgoing DeathLink. Amnesty (forgive the first N per session) and cooldown are options.

## Goals

`story` (beat all five Idol missions, default), `mission_count`, `challenges`, `memorabilia`, `five_star_story` (5 stars on every Idol
mission) or `story_and_challenges`, with a goal count where it applies.

## The player YAML

`Dead as Disco.yaml` is generated with Archipelago's own template generator, so it looks like every other game's: grouped sections
(**Goal and Missions**, **Shuffled Rewards**, **What Counts as a Check**, **Filler and Traps**, **DeathLink**), every option explained in
plain language with its choices and weights, and `requires:` pinning Archipelago 0.6.8 and this world version. Theme groups work in
`exclude_locations` / `priority_locations` (Memorabilia, Challenges, Quests, Cosmetic Purchases, Dance Purchases, Story Completions,
Achievements, Song Completions, Skill Nodes, Upgrade Nodes, Star Ratings, Difficulty Checks, ...) and `local_items` / `non_local_items`
(Idol Powers, Active Skills, Passive Upgrades, Mission Access, Traps, Filler).

## The app

`DeadAsDiscoAP.exe` is a one-window launcher: type the server, your slot name and the password, tick that Steam Cloud is off, press
**PLAY**. It sets itself up, creates a separate Archipelago profile for each multiworld (you play the short tutorial once), starts the
game with the mod, connects to the server, and shows an Archipelago-style console with received items, chat and `!hint` / `!help`.

**Your real saves are never played on.** They are moved aside for the session and put back with a hash check, with a full backup kept;
**Recover saves** fixes any interrupted session. Nothing stays installed in the game folder between sessions.

## Changes since 0.5.6 (app and mod fixes; the world is still 0.5.5)

- **Fixes "CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED ... certificate has expired" when connecting to archipelago.gg.** Some PCs have an out-of-date Windows certificate store and rejected the server's valid certificate. The app now falls back to its own bundled trusted roots (still checking the server and its name), tries the secure address first for archipelago.gg, and tells you to check the PC's date and time if the certificate is still rejected.

- **Fixes "ERROR: SaveError('Unsupported save header')" after the game update.** The update stamps every save it writes with a new engine version, and the app only accepted the old one, so it could not read the profile after the tutorial. Saves written by both game builds are read now (checked against real saves from both).

- **Supports the new Dead as Disco update (Steam build 25772865).** The update changed combat and boss behaviour and re-cooked many
  levels; the game's configuration, text, item and location definitions are byte-identical, so no multiworld is affected. Builds
  25772865 and 25647873 are both accepted.
- **A slow moment no longer ends the session.** A single slow moment (such as loading the hub on a slower PC) used to end the session
  with "The game bridge stopped itself (callback-time-budget)". The bridge now backs off and only stops on a real error or a stall of
  several seconds, and the message says exactly which it was and where to find the details file.
- **The window now finds Dead as Disco wherever Steam is installed** (for example `F:\Steam`) and asks for the game folder if it still
  cannot find it, instead of crashing with "Dead as Disco install not found".
- Everything from 0.5.6 stays: no lock-out after closing the game at the wrong moment, and errors show up immediately.
- **Multiworlds generated with 0.5.5 keep working**: the `dead_as_disco.apworld` and YAML inside this zip are still version 0.5.5. Only
  the app and the mod changed: replace the app folder and press PLAY. Progress lives in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DeadAsDiscoAP` and is not touched.

## Known limits

- **Fan Packs are experimental.** They run in a protected test mode until one live run confirms the exact fan amounts.
- **Not yet confirmed in live play:** memorabilia pickup checks, the Dex / Prophet / Dahlia Access items individually, Silence cleanup on
  death or level transition, Half Heart at very low health, the story victory goal, and multi-hour sessions.
- **After the tutorial you must quit the game and close Steam once** so the Archipelago game can start; an automatic restart is planned.
- Steam Cloud must be off for the game while you play, and the a supported game build is required.
- **Not possible:** a stamina trap (the game has no stamina), shuffling the cosmetic/dance rewards themselves (only their purchases are checks).
  50 song-related records are not checks because their song data cannot be tied to a visible, unlockable song.
- Multiworlds generated with an older, pre-release version are not compatible; generate a new one.

## Verified in live play

Skill-node purchases as checks with the vanilla reward withheld, Archipelago skill delivery with automatic equip, mission access, powers
gated until delivered, Half Heart and Silence, DeathLink in both directions, challenges, persistence across sessions, and save restoration
after every session. Details: [docs/FINAL_AUDIT.md](../FINAL_AUDIT.md).
